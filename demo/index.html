Farm-to-Shelf Beer Game

A supply chain simulation that starts at the farm: farm → factory → distributor → wholesaler → retailer → customers. Farmers plant once a season, harvests vary, crops spoil and prices swing. Compare two ways of running the chain and see who wins, who loses and who carries the risk.
